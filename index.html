About Siddharth Sagar – Magician in Patna

Hi, I'm Siddharth Sagar, a professional magician from Patna, Bihar. I turn ordinary moments into things people keep talking about long after the show is over. My performances blend sleight of hand, mentalism, stage illusions, and close-up magic with humour and storytelling, so audiences of every age laugh, gasp, and stay hooked from the first trick to the last.

I've performed at weddings, birthday parties, corporate events, school programmes, festivals, and stage shows across Patna and many districts of Bihar. Wherever I go, I bring the same energy: a show built around the audience, with plenty of participation and a few surprises nobody sees coming.

For me, magic isn't just about tricks. It's about wonder, that moment when a whole room forgets everything else and simply believes in the impossible. I design every performance to create that feeling, so the memories last long after the applause.

I'm available for private events, corporate functions, and stage shows in Patna and across Bihar.

Looking for a magician in Patna for your next event? Book me and give your guests something they'll never forget.
